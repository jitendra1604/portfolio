I've been thinking about writing blogs for a long time, but I always felt like I didn't have anything important enough to say.

The hardest part was never creating a website or writing the words. It was believing that my thoughts are worth sharing. But then I realised — I don't need to wait until I know everything. I can just start from where I am and learn along the way. 🌱

I first wanted to start blogging back in engineering. I've always believed that if you're able to explain what's going on in your mind, you can solve a lot of problems by yourself. Sometimes we have a lot of thoughts, but they're not clear. Writing helps me put those thoughts in one place and understand them better.

For me, blogging isn't only about sharing knowledge with others. It's also a way to improve my thinking and communication. ✍️ When I write about something, I get to know what I actually understand and what I still need to learn.

I feel that when people are able to follow my thought process, it means I'm getting better at presenting my ideas. And I believe this will help me in the future — whether it's at work, solving problems, working with a team, or building something of my own. 💡

This is my first step. I may not be perfect, and I may not have all the answers right now, but I'm ready to learn, write, and share my journey. 🚀
